# GPT Scientific Writing

Use this process for GPT drafting and substantive revision. It is distilled from
recurring patterns in the user's GPT writing sessions; the same patterns were not
observed in their Claude sessions.

## 1. Establish the question and audience

Material-driven drafts explain what was done before establishing why readers
should care. Begin with a one-page argument linking the intended readers, the
scientific question, the strategy for answering it, the supporting evidence, and
the significance within the demonstrated scope. Use this argument to organize
paragraphs and figures. Each paragraph advances a step in the reasoning; each
main figure answers a scientific question.

## 2. Give contributions and validation distinct roles

When implementation and evaluation receive the same emphasis as the contribution,
the advance becomes hard to locate. State the contribution as a change in
understanding, capability, or practice. Place each evaluation under the claim it
tests. Describe methods through their role in producing and interpreting that
evidence, with supporting reproducibility records at a further level. Keep mixed
results and scientifically consequential approaches in this hierarchy: they help
establish what the contribution actually supports.

## 3. Select information by its argumentative value

Completeness-driven writing gives every available detail space in the paper. For
each item, ask which reader question it answers and whether it is needed to
interpret, test, or reproduce a claim. Use the main text for the argument, Methods
for scientifically necessary detail, and supporting materials for operational
records. Select abstract terminology by the shared knowledge of the field's broad
readership and the finding's explanatory needs. This relevance judgment makes
rigor compatible with clarity.

## 4. Translate evidence into scientific statements

Direct transfer from source materials can preserve operational detail while
leaving the inference implicit. State the observation, comparison conditions, and
conclusion it supports. Translate internal records into relevant data versions,
parameter choices, sampling definitions, and comparison conditions. Make the
tested scope part of the claim and develop remaining limitations coherently in
Discussion. A precise claim communicates a useful finding with its boundaries.

## 5. Revise the argument as a whole

Repeated sentence-level repairs can produce disconnected emphases and obscure
the original question. When revisions drift, return to the one-page argument,
map claims to evidence, and rebuild the paragraph and figure sequence. Replace
superseded claims and merge repeated caveats in place; Git retains draft history.
Redraft affected sections from that structure, using the previous draft for verified
facts and citations. Resume polishing once the reasoning is coherent; apply the
parent workflow's evidence-gap and review rules.

## 6. Validate different kinds of quality separately

Mechanical success can coexist with weak inference or an unreadable argument.
Give each pass its own verdict and unresolved issues:

| Pass | Acceptance question |
| --- | --- |
| Scientific evidence | Do the design, comparisons, and observations support accurate claims, including mixed results and inferential limits? Do statistical analyses fit the sampling units and assumptions? |
| Reader understanding and narrative | Can intended readers recover the question, strategy, evidence, and significance from the abstract and main figures, then follow the paragraph sequence? |
| Mechanical integrity | Do quantities, units, figure references, and citations match their sources, and does the document compile correctly? |

Use the scientific and narrative verdicts to decide whether the paper is effective;
use mechanical checks to verify the document's consistency and build integrity.
