## Full-text evidence audit

### Dual-Mode Oscillations

What supports top-end quality independently of the front-cover label:[^dual-mode-article]

- The central contribution is integrated rather than additive: one CMOS-compatible SiOx threshold-switching device is deliberately tuned to provide two controllable oscillation modes and support three distinct computing uses (oscillatory neural networks, probabilistic bits, and true random generation).
- The causal chain is unusually traceable. The paper links deposition gas ratio to oxygen-vacancy concentration, switching-parameter variability, charging/discharging regimes, observed oscillation behavior, and downstream system behavior. TEM, XPS, electrical measurements, equations, and application simulations play complementary roles in that chain.
- Validation is staged and quantitative: three deposition ratios, 200 switching cycles, measurements across five cells, voltage-dependent operating maps, noisy-pattern recognition, vehicle-routing optimization, entropy analysis, and the complete NIST SP 800-22 suite.
- It reports a meaningful failure rather than hiding it. A single entropy source has high Shannon entropy yet fails several NIST tests; the authors identify residual periodicity, introduce five-input XOR postprocessing, show that extreme input imbalance still fails, define the successful `P1 = 0.35-0.65` operating window, and finally validate a 100 Mbit stream against all 15 tests.
- Figures carry the reasoning from mechanism through failure and correction; the conclusion mirrors the demonstrated sequence rather than expanding beyond it.

Residual limitations remain visible: the longest-bitstream validation and several applications are simulation-based rather than full hardware demonstrations, only five cells are reported for one reproducibility check, and data are available by request rather than deposited. These do not erase the unusually coherent, multi-level evidence chain, but they remain legitimate review comments.

### RAMS

What supports top-end quality independently of the back-cover label:[^rams-article]

- The method addresses a clear bottleneck—finding informative samples in high-dimensional PDE and operator-learning spaces—using a simple, reusable abstraction: treat samples as trainable variables and move them by adversarial gradients toward high-residual regions.
- The paper makes the method inspectable through equations and Algorithms 2-6, then shows how it composes with three non-adaptive samplers and three adaptive samplers rather than presenting a single bespoke pipeline.
- The empirical matrix is broad and internally consistent: ten examples span PINNs, physics-informed operator learning, and data-driven operator learning; tests include one- and two-dimensional problems, dimensions up to 10, discontinuities, interpolation and extrapolation settings, and multiple sample budgets.
- Comparisons report more than wins: 3-10 independent runs depending on the experiment, standard-deviation bars, parameter sensitivity, training cost, less than 2% overhead in several PINN cases, up to 63% error reduction in one data-driven setting, and performance comparable to DAS2 with about 3% of its samples in the reported dynamic-system comparison.
- The appendix supplies network architectures and sampling/training settings, the projector component is checked by an ablation-like comparison, and public code is linked. This makes the main claims easier to challenge and reproduce.

Residual limitations remain visible: the examples are numerical PDE systems rather than real experimental deployments, broader foundation-model use is future work, and the formal data statement says data are available on request despite public code. These are bounded limitations rather than defects in the tested central claim.

## Within-journal use and calibration limit

These two records establish audited top-end evidence for *Advanced Intelligent Discovery* only. Within the pair, Dual-Mode is especially informative for a mechanism-to-application chain that includes a failed randomness test and a bounded correction, while RAMS is especially informative for breadth across learning settings, repeated trials, efficiency reporting, and reusable algorithms. Page count, number of figures, topic prestige, and cover artwork are not themselves evidence of excellence.

This file does not establish the journal's `6-9` ladder because it does not yet contain comparable ordinary articles from the same journal. When such controls are added, compare them with these exemplars using localized full-text evidence. Until then, leave those bands under-calibrated and do not use these cover papers to set another journal's numeric thresholds.
